Data: In an oak tree just coming out in leaf up 10 feet along the bed of a dry canyon. The tree was bushy branching starting at 2 feet. The nest was conspicuous, being seen by me 40 feet away after being passed by my two companions. The bird sat very tightly and did not flush until I was at the nest when it stepped off but soon took on a pugnacious look, its crest feathers thrown forward and and called “Jay” “Jay” very shrilly. There were many old nests about and two sets of 3 were located nearby only one of which was taken (3/3) making it a small colony. It is natural the jays should be nesting close together here as it was the only trees and shrubs for miles around. The platform of the nest, wedged in a spreading crotch was made up of small pencil sized twigs on which was placed a nicely moulded nest of black rootlets, those on the inside being very fine.
